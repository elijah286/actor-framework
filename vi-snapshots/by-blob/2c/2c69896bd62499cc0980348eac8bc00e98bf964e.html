Actor Framework.lvlib:Actor.lvclass:Stop Autostop Nested Actors.vi

<p>(<strong>Filename</strong>: <span class="dnl">Actor Framework.lvlib:Actor.lvclass:Stop Autostop Nested Actors.vi</span>)</p>

<p>Sends a normal Stop message to any auto-stop nested actors that were launched by an actor but have not yet sent back their Last Ack messages. Use this VI when you want to stop all of an actor's nested actors, but not the actor itself.</p>
