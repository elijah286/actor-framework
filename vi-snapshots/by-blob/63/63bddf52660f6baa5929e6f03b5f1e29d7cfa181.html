Actor Framework.lvlib:Stop Msg.lvclass:Send Emergency Stop.vi

<p>(<strong>Filename</strong>: <span class="dnl">Actor Framework.lvlib:Stop Msg.lvclass:Send Emergency Stop.vi</span>)</p>

<p>Sends an Emergency Stop message to an actor, triggering the actor to shut down as quickly as possible. This message has critical priority, meaning it will be processed before all messages that are already in the queue.</p>
