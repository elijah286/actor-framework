AF Specialty Messages.lvlib:Batch Msg.lvclass:Send Batch.vi

<p>(<strong>Filename</strong>: <span class="dnl">Actor Framework.lvlib:Batch Msg.lvclass:Send Batch.vi</span>)</p>

<p>Sends multiple messages in a batch to an actor. All messages in the batch are given the same priority. No additional messages of the same priority will be processed by the actor in between the messages in the batch. However, messages with a higher priority than the batch will be processed before the batch itself.</p>
